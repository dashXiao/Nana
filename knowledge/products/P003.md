## Description
An ergonomic chair for long hours at a desk, with adjustable lumbar support, headrest, and armrests.

## Features
- Adaptive lumbar support
- 4D adjustable armrests
- Three-section headrest
- Breathable mesh backrest
- Quiet PU casters

## Specifications
- Weight capacity: 150 kg
- Adjustable seat height: 46–56 cm
- Backrest angle: 90°–135°
- Materials: high-resilience foam and mesh
- Colors: Graphite Black / Gray Blue

## Recommended use
For users who spend at least four hours a day at a desk. Users taller than 1.9 m should choose the version with the extended headrest instead of the standard version.
