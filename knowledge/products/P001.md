## Description
Entry-level true wireless earbuds with a semi-in-ear design for everyday commuting and calls.

## Features
- Bluetooth 5.3 with a stable, low-latency connection
- 6 hours of playback per charge; 28 hours total with the charging case
- AI noise reduction for calls
- IPX4 splash resistance for sweat and light rain

## Specifications
- Weight per earbud: 4.5 g
- Charging port: USB-C
- Bluetooth version: 5.3
- Latency: 60 ms in gaming mode
- Colors: Ceramic White / Obsidian Black

## Recommended use
Commuting, calls, and outdoor walks. Not recommended for intense exercise or professional music applications.
