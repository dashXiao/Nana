## Description
A smartwatch with around-the-clock health monitoring and more than 120 workout modes.

## Features
- Continuous heart rate, blood oxygen, and sleep monitoring
- 120+ workout modes and built-in GPS
- Water resistance to 50 meters; suitable for swimming
- Message notifications and incoming call alerts
- 14-day battery life under typical use

## Specifications
- Display: 1.43-inch AMOLED
- Weight: 32 g without the strap
- Strap options: silicone / leather
- Compatibility: iOS 13+ / Android 8+
- Colors: Starry Black / Moonlight Silver

## Recommended use
Everyday health tracking, running, cycling, and swimming. Not suitable for diving.
